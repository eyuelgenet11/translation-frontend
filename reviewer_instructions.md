# Information for Google Play Reviewers

Thank you for reviewing the Geez Script Translation Marketplace app.

Since our app facilitates real-world translation services (done by human experts), it involves real payments (which use external gateways under the physical services exemption).

To help you review the full functionality of the app without needing to make a real payment, we have provided a test account loaded with "Loyalty Points". You can use these points to bypass the payment gateways and simulate a complete translation transaction.

## Test Credentials
- **Email:** reviewer@geezmarketplace.com
- **Password:** Reviewer2026!

## Test Flow (How to verify full functionality):
1. **Login** using the credentials above.
2. Tap the **Upload Document** button (+) on the home screen.
3. Select "English" to "Amharic", choose a deadline, and tap "Upload PDF". (You can upload any placeholder file or use the camera to take a picture of a document).
4. Tap **Submit Order**. 
5. The status will initially be "Finding Translator". Because human translators are offline, please wait a moment while the admin system (or simulated backend) updates the status to "Price Quoted".
6. Once the status says **"Down Payment Needed"**, open the Job Details screen.
7. Instead of submitting a receipt, tap the **"Pay with Points"** button. This will use your test account's Loyalty Points to bypass the payment gateway.
8. The project will move to "In Progress", then "Completed".
9. Once "Completed", you can download the final placeholder file.

## Settings & Compliance
To verify our data safety compliance:
- Navigate to the **Profile Tab** -> **Settings (Gear Icon)**.
- Under **Support & Legal**, you will find our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
- Under **About**, you can find the **Delete Account** button, which initiates account deletion data purging within 30 days as requested by Google Play Data Safety guidelines.

If you have any questions during your review, please contact support@geezmarketplace.com.
