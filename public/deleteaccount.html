Request Account & Data Deletion

If you wish to permanently delete your Tirgum Sra account and all associated data, you have two options:

Option 1: In-App Deletion (Recommended)

You can instantly delete your account directly within the mobile application:

  1. Open the Tirgum Sra app and log in.
  2. Navigate to the Settings screen.
  3. Scroll down and tap on Delete Account.
  4. Confirm your decision. Your account and all associated data will be deleted immediately.

Option 2: Email Request

If you no longer have the app installed or cannot log in, you can submit an account deletion request using the form below. Please provide the email address associated with your account.

Note: Email deletion requests are processed manually within 30 days. This action is irreversible and all your data, including job history and payment details, will be permanently removed.
